## 1. Premise and player

**Logline:** Eleven days after Wren vanished, her phone starts copying itself onto yours. Every notification you get, someone else might be getting too.

**You (John)**, 24, renameable. You tested pedestrian bridges. One reading didn't add up. You said nothing. The colleague who spoke up was let go. You moved here eight months ago to be someone nobody asks anything of. Wren noticed.

Last message, 11:47 PM on the 13th: *still up? come by, I'll leave the door open.* Delivered. Never read. You went upstairs. Wren stood in your kitchen.

**Calendar.** First date: 22nd of last month. Night she vanished: the 13th (exactly three weeks later). Episode 1 starts 3:07 AM on the 24th. The story runs to just after midnight on the 26th. Lock-screen: **Hours since last read: 264.**

**Public deadline: Full Pool Day.** Saturday, winter high mark, brass band, Everett Reed signs the 2024 extension. Ardis needs Wren silent before then. The dam cannot survive that water.

## 2. The water

- Two-tone siren ~30 seconds before the gates open. Website: daylight tests only.
- Gates open by key at the Authority office, fifty metres from the boom shed. Only the Chair holds the key.
- Boom cable strikes the brass buoy bell once at open, once at close. A full release: about 0.4 m in nine minutes.
- Official record: release log, signed by **Councilwoman Ardis Locke**.
- Legend: drowned frost-relays ringing on foggy nights. It is a log of every night release.

## 4. The truth

The 2006 inspection said **FAIL**: seepage under the spillway wall. The filed copy said **PASS**. Night releases keep the pool below the level the wall can't hold.

Caleb took FAIL to Ardis. She asked him to the spillway house on 18 November "with the engineer." The engineer never came. At **23:40** she ran a night release. His own sensor logged it. The next week they flooded the east basin orchard - including three frost-relay posts that were never salvaged.

Wren found the same report. Night of the 11th she watched a release from the boom shed. Night of the 13th a dark car came for her at the dock.

**Night of the 13th:** 11:31 dock photo; 11:38 texts; 11:41 call (siren already on, bell 0:31 = gates opening); 11:41:32 sensor drop; 11:47 unread message; 11:50 gates close (second bell); 11:52 wifi link in your kitchen; 12:02 locker photo.

Same script twenty years apart, one minute off.

## 8. What the game is about

You were never hired. You were implicated. You spent your life avoiding moments like this, and Wren picked you anyway. The kindest voice in town benefits from a book that says the water never moves at night. The ghost story is the crime scene. John becomes a witness who will not look away, and says it out loud.
